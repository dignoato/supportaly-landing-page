ONE IDEA / THREE THINGS TO CLARIFY

“Can we remove this wall?”

What you mean

A larger, brighter room for everyday family life. Explain what you want to achieve, rather than assuming one solution.

What your architect needs to check

Whether the wall is structural, what the building allows and which approvals, costs and technical implications need assessment.

What you clarify together

Which information is missing, who will check it and what must be understood before choosing a solution.

HOW TO USE THE KITPREPARE → CLARIFY → REVIEW

Prepare your brief.
Review it together.

You can begin before appointing an architect. Use the information you have, leave unanswered questions open and bring your preparation into the first professional conversation.

1

Prepare

Make a working copy of the Client Workbook. Describe the property, how you want to use the home, the changes you imagine, your budget limits and important dates. Write in your own words.

2

Clarify

Separate what you know from wishes, assumptions and questions needing professional advice. If others share the decisions, review your priorities together and mark where you still need to agree.

3

Review

Share the workbook with your architect or geometra before or during the first meeting. Use the Architect Handover together to record open questions, responsibilities and next steps. Technical checks and professional advice remain their responsibility.

Why I created
Supportaly.

Clarity before commitment.

I’m an Italian architect and project manager with experience in Italy and Germany.

I’ve seen international clients struggle too often with unclear expectations, language barriers and misunderstandings about their renovation in Italy. I’ve also seen Italian professionals face the same difficulties from the other side of the conversation.

THE CLARITY KITA CLEARER WAY TO BEGIN

WHY PREPARATION MATTERS

Start with a clearer brief, not more assumptions.

The six documents help turn the things you know about your property and future home into information you can share, discuss and develop with your Italian architect or geometra.

You will still face unknown conditions, technical questions and decisions that require professional advice. Preparing early makes it easier to recognise those open points instead of treating assumptions as settled facts.

What matters is knowing what remains open.

Supportaly gives both sides a common starting reference, so each conversation can focus on what needs to be understood, checked or decided next.

BEFORE YOU BEGINUSEFUL THINGS TO KNOW

YOUR QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Explore the Clarity Kit
01 — What will the kit help me prepare?+

A practical starting brief for your renovation in Italy. The six PDFs help you describe your property, future home, priorities, financial limits and open questions, then bring that information into the conversation with your architect or geometra.

02 — When should I start using it?+

You can start before choosing an architect. It is useful to prepare what you know and identify your questions before that first conversation.

Your architect will then need to review the information and carry out the checks relevant to your project.

03 — What if much of my project is still uncertain?+

You can still begin. You do not need to know the required permits, exact construction costs or whether a particular design idea is feasible.

The workbook provides space to record unanswered questions so your architect can help you understand what needs checking.

04 — Which parts of the kit are bilingual?+

You complete the Client Workbook in English. The kit also contains English/Italian tools for recording important discussions and preparing messages for your Italian professional.

The six PDFs are not all fully translated into both languages.

05 — Can my architect work with these documents?+

Yes. The completed documents are intended to be discussed and shared with the professionals working on your own property project.

The personal licence covers your project. Repeated commercial use of the blank templates for different clients requires separate authorisation.

06 — How do I complete and use the files?+

The kit contains six PDF files. The Client Workbook and professional records have fillable fields; the bilingual Message Starters can be copied and adapted to your situation.

Use a PDF reader that supports interactive form fields.

07 — Who checks whether my renovation is possible?+

Your qualified local professionals must carry out the necessary checks. The kit does not establish feasibility, verify planning or cadastral compliance, determine permits, prepare technical drawings or calculate project costs.

Their professional advice, services and responsibilities remain essential to the project.
