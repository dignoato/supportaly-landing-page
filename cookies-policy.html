This Cookie Policy explains how Supportaly uses cookies and similar technologies on www.supportaly.com, and how visitors can manage their preferences.

It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

Cookies are small pieces of information stored on your device when you visit a website.

They may help a website operate correctly, remember preferences, maintain secure sessions or understand how visitors interact with its pages.

Similar technologies may include local storage, tracking pixels and other tools that store or access information on a device.

Depending on the features enabled, Supportaly may use the following categories of technologies.

Strictly necessary technologies

These support essential website functionality, such as security, login sessions, shopping-cart operations, checkout and cookie-preference management.

Where the relevant legal exception applies, they do not require prior cookie consent.

Preferences and functionality

These may remember optional settings that improve your experience.

Analytics

If enabled, analytics technologies may help us understand website usage and identify areas for improvement.

Marketing and advertising

If enabled, advertising technologies may be used to measure campaign performance or support relevant advertising.

Optional technologies are activated only where a valid legal basis and any required consent exist.

Our website uses Wix-based website services.

Depending on the configured integrations, cookies or related technologies may also be set by payment, authentication, delivery, analytics or other third-party services.

The final list of providers, purposes and retention periods will be published once verified against the live website configuration.

Where non-essential technologies are used, visitors will be offered appropriate choices before those technologies are activated.

Where required, you will be able to:

  • Accept optional cookies.
  • Reject optional cookies.
  • Adjust your preferences.
  • Withdraw consent later.

Withdrawing consent will not affect processing that was lawful before the withdrawal.

You may also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking necessary functionality may affect account access, checkout or other site features.
