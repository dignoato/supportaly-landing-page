Refund & Withdrawal Policy

Supportaly — www.supportaly.com

*Last updated: [Publication date]*

Supportaly offers downloadable digital products, including the Supportaly Clarity Kit.

We want customers to understand their purchase and their rights before downloading a product.

Consumers purchasing online generally have a statutory right to withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days, subject to applicable exceptions.

For digital content supplied without a physical medium, this right may expire when the supply begins if the consumer has:

  1. Expressly consented to delivery beginning before the withdrawal period expires.
  2. Acknowledged that the right of withdrawal will be lost when supply begins.
  3. Received the legally required confirmation of the agreement and consent.

Where these legal conditions have not been met, the statutory withdrawal right remains available according to applicable law.

If you are entitled to withdraw, you may notify us through a clear statement sent to:

info@supportaly.com

You may use the model withdrawal form provided with your order documentation, but using the form is not mandatory.

Where withdrawal is valid, we will reimburse payments in accordance with the applicable statutory requirements.

Your statutory rights are not affected by the expiry of the withdrawal right.

If a purchased product is defective, cannot be accessed, is incomplete or does not conform to its description, please contact us.

Where required by law, we will provide appropriate remedies, which may include making the product conform, replacement access, a price reduction or termination of the contract and reimbursement.

If you have difficulty downloading or opening a file, contact info@supportaly.com with your order details and a description of the problem.

We will review the issue and provide assistance or an appropriate remedy.

Where the statutory withdrawal right has lawfully expired and no other legal right applies, we are not obliged to offer a refund solely because the customer changes their mind.

We may review individual requests at our discretion. This does not affect mandatory consumer rights.

Customers outside the EU may have additional or different mandatory rights under applicable local law.

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude consumer rights that cannot lawfully be restricted.

For withdrawals, refunds, defective files or delivery problems:

info@supportaly.com
